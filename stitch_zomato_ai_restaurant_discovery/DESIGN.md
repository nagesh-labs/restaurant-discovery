## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, sensory dark-mode language tailored for intelligent culinary discovery in high-density urban hubs like Bangalore. The experience merges the warmth, vitality, and indulgence of premier dining with the analytical precision of generative intelligence. 

The aesthetic philosophy is **Atmospheric Glassmorphism meets Precision Cyber-Dining**:
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Surfaces rely on deep charcoal foundations with localized dynamic lighting—ambient ruby halos for sensory resonance and electric cyan highlights for real-time computational insights.
- **Micro-Precision Engineering:** Complex metadata (such as hyperlocal traffic times, price-for-two in INR, dietary tags, and AI reasoning fragments) are rendered with surgical clarity using monospaced or tabular numeric alignment, pill-shaped badges, and frosted, translucent surfaces.
- **Tactile Depth:** Interfaces favor layered elevation through structural frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), luminous 1px gradient borders, and soft neon penumbras that signal live updates, kitchen availability, and predictive recommendations.

## Colors

The palette balances intense brand equity with high-contrast night-time legibility and technical sophistication.

### Core Tonal Strategy
- **Base Canvas:** `#0D0F12` (Onyx Void) acts as the primary background viewport, eliminating visual fatigue and allowing vibrant imagery to anchor the eye.
- **Elevated Canvas:** `#14171F` (Deep Charcoal) forms secondary structural containers, headers, and bottom navigation docking bars.
- **Glass Surfaces:** `#1A1E29` at `65%` to `80%` opacity paired with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders creates distinct frosted glass planes that sit comfortably above vivid hero photography.
- **Brand Crimson Vector:** `#E23744` (Zomato Vivid Crimson) gradients smoothly into `#FF5722` (Searing Coral) to drive high-intent calls to action, active tabs, and primary sensory elements.
- **Intelligence Signal:** `#38BDF8` (Electric Cyan) is reserved strictly for algorithmic discovery: natural-language reasoning callouts, taste-compatibility metrics, dynamic route latency tags across Bangalore corridors (e.g., Indiranagar to Koramangala), and query token pills.
- **Culinary Validation & Tiering:** `#10B981` (Emerald) denotes elite dining ratings (4.5+ ★) and verified food-safety scores. `#F59E0B` (Amber Gold) represents curated collections, Michelin-style culinary achievements, and elite membership status.
- **Typography Scale:** `#F8FAFC` (Text Primary, 95% opacity), `#94A3B8` (Text Secondary, 65% opacity), and `#64748B` (Text Muted, 40% opacity).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is designed around dual-engine functional rendering:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers humanist warmth, modern rounded forms, and high-impact visual presence across restaurant titles, marketing headers, and curated editorial content.
- **Inter** handles dense data surfaces, UI labels, badge overlays, and financial metrics. Inter's OpenType tabular numeric figures (`tnum`, `cv05`) ensure that Indian Rupee amounts (`₹`), review counts, timestamps, and real-time delivery distances remain vertically and horizontally aligned across varying dynamic data.

### Formatting Rules
- All currency representations must use the explicit Rupee glyph `₹` followed immediately by the tabular numeric amount with zero kerning gap (e.g., `₹850 for two`).
- AI reasoning notes and contextual citations should be styled with `body-sm` using an italic posture and Cyan-tinted text (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.9)`).
- Micro-labels inside pills and status badges must enforce uppercase transformation (`text-transform: uppercase`) paired with `label-sm` to maintain balance alongside heavy photographic textures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system employs a responsive fluid-grid structure customized for mobile-first handheld execution with seamless expansion to wide-screen discovery dashboards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Cards, AI prompt bars, and restaurant feeds stretch full-width to maximize touch real estate.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Discovery tiles organize into 2-column masonry grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at an absolute max-width of `1360px` with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates a persistent AI conversational side-rail alongside a 3-column discovery matrix.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Micro-Gaps (`space-xs`, 4px):** Icon-to-label offsets, inline rating stars, and status indicators.
- **Component Padding (`space-sm`, 8px & `space-md`, 16px):** Badge insets, standard button vertical/horizontal padding, and internal card content stacking.
- **Section Rhythm (`space-lg`, 24px & `space-xl`, 40px):** Separation between thematic sections (e.g., "Trending in Indiranagar" vs. "AI Taste Match for You").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on physical transparency and calibrated luminescence rather than traditional drop shadows, maintaining crisp delineation against dark canvases.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0D0F12`. Default viewport background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Recessed/Muted Trays):** `#14171F` with no blur. Used for search filter groupings and inactive category strips.
- **Surface Level 2 (Glass Cards):** Background `rgba(26, 30, 41, 0.75)` supported by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Floating Active Modals & Prompts):** Background `rgba(30, 36, 51, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.

### Neon Halo & Intent Glows
- **AI Focus Glow:** Interactive elements actively running predictive processing feature a continuous border gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.6), rgba(226, 55, 68, 0.6))` accompanied by an outer box-shadow: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **Zomato Pulse Glow:** Primary actions and promotional cards incorporate an accent under-glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(226, 55, 68, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses a `Level 2 (Rounded)` architecture balanced by full pill closures for micro-interactive units:
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Base radius of `1rem` (16px, `rounded-lg`) produces a friendly yet clean and structured framing for content.
- **Floating Overlays & AI Prompt Sheets:** `1.5rem` (24px, `rounded-xl`) for soft tactile grounding when sweeping sheets up from the base of the screen.
- **Interactive Badges, Metric Chips & Primary CTAs:** Fully rounded pill styling (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). Pills evoke sensory food capsules, providing visual contrast against large photographic cards.
- **Input Fields & Text Areas:** `0.75rem` (12px) to provide a grounded, high-precision interface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Gradient background from `#E23744` to `#FF5722`. High-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`, `label-md`). Padding: `12px 24px`. Full pill border radius (`rounded-full`). Ambient red glow on hover/active states.
- **Secondary Glass Button:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F8FAFC`. Active state transitions to background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **AI Action Button:** Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`, text `#38BDF8`. Features a dynamic sparkle icon prefixed to the label.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chip (Default):** Surface `#14171F`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#94A3B8`. Padding: `6px 14px`.
- **Filter Chip (Selected):** Crimson background `#E23744`, border `1px solid transparent`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **AI Context Pill:** Translucent electric cyan tint (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`), text `#38BDF8`, paired with a micro-pulsing indicator dot.

### Restaurant Cards
- Multi-layered glass surface (`#1A1E29` at `75%` blur).
- Aspect ratio of `16:9` for cover media with an inward linear vignette (`linear-gradient(to top, rgba(26, 30, 41, 0.95), transparent 60%)`).
- Floating badges: Top-right incorporates Emerald rating pill (`#10B981`, white bold text). Top-left displays AI Match Score pill (`rgba(13, 15, 18, 0.8)` background, Cyan accent border, e.g., "96% Taste Match").
- Lower interior section: Title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/cuisines in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`), and tabular pricing string (`₹1,200 for two`).

### AI Reasoning Quote Box
- Embedded inside cards or modal summaries to communicate generative recommendations.
- Styled as an inset panel: Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04)`, left border accent `2px solid #38BDF8`.
- Accommodates Bangalore-specific natural language insights (e.g., *"Perfect for quiet work sessions in Indiranagar; craft sourdough and single-origin pour-overs verified by 140+ recent reviews."*).

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Search & Prompt Input:** Height `52px`, pill or 12px rounded, background `rgba(20, 23, 31, 0.85)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state introduces a glowing perimeter of `#38BDF8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-contrast rounded squares (4px) or circles. Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked: Solid `#E23744` fill featuring an interior crisp white glyph.
